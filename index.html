About Us

Growing together in faith and community

Christian Club meetings include a variety of events: short sermons from guest speakers, volunteer events, and bible study! We meet for an hour during access and share our faith with food and friends. Join us for a safe space and a good time!

Our Mission: The mission of Emerald Christian Club is to provide a safe space for Christians to grow in faith and give back to the community.
